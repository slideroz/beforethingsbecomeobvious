I've never been handed a clear brief. Teams rarely lack momentum; what's missing is agreement about what they're building, and momentum makes that gap expensive. Clarity is what's left after the research, the arguments, and the three versions I threw away.

Craft is attention to the parts nobody names — continuity, rhythm, the detail that makes something feel intuitive instead of explained. Mostly it's invisible until it's missing. I still build things with my own hands, which keeps me honest about what's actually possible.

{{ tw.s4.head }}{{ tw.s4.tail }}

Most of my work is deciding — what to build, what to leave out, what the interface is allowed to imply. In regulated work those decisions get read back to me by legal, privacy, security and the people who have to live with them. So I design them to hold up when they're read.
